# Hi-Med Prepas: Website Summary for the Promo Video

## 1. What the site is for

Hi-Med Prepas is a national center in Cameroon for career orientation and preparation for competitive entrance exams (the *concours*). The website exists to:

1. **Build trust:** results since 2015, pass rate, student testimonials.
2. **Show what is offered:** four program tracks and the schools each one targets.
3. **Show where classes happen:** centers across 5 regions, plus online classes.
4. **Get sign-ups:** almost every section leads to a **"Pre-Register Now"** button that opens a short registration form.

## 2. Who it is for

- **Students** finishing secondary school who want to pass a competitive entrance exam for Medicine, Engineering, Nursing or Teaching schools in Cameroon.
- **Parents**, who often choose and pay for the preparation and look for proof of results.

Tone: professional, reassuring and ambitious, aimed at students and parents.

## 3. Site structure

The site is **one long landing page** with nine sections, in this order, plus a **pre-registration form** that pops up over the page. There are no other pages. The menu links scroll down to the matching section.

### 4.10 Pre-registration form (pop-up)

## 6. Colours and fonts

**Font:** **Plus Jakarta Sans** (Google Fonts), the only font on the site. Headlines are extra-bold with tight letter spacing; small labels are uppercase with wide spacing.

**Colours** (taken from the logo):

| Colour | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Blue | `#0056B3` | Main buttons, highlights, active links, icons |
| Deep Navy | `#0A1C2E` | Headings, statistics band, footer, hero gradient |
| Accent Red | `#C1121F` | Button hover, "Since 2015" badge, milestone dots, underline, small accents |
| Off-White | `#F8F9FA` | Background of alternating sections |
| Dark Slate | `#1E293B` | Body text |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Main backgrounds, text on dark sections |
| Light Blue | `#A6CBF2` | "National" in the hero, accents on dark backgrounds |

**"Hi-Med" wordmark gradient** (measured from the logo lettering, left to right): `#2171B9` → `#0D4D8B` → `#0A1C2E`. On dark backgrounds the wordmark fades from white to light blue instead.

**Signature gradient:** Primary Blue `#0056B3` → Deep Navy `#0A1C2E`, used on the hero and the final banner.
